About replay dates

Most replay dates are exact. Replays marked ≈ week of … have an estimated date, usually within a few days of the real one.

Where dates come from

bonk.io doesn't publish when a replay was uploaded, so Bonkverse records the date itself. Its replay collector fetches each new replay within minutes of the game ending and saves the time it was collected. That time is the date you see.

Every replay gets the next ID number in its cycle, starting from 1 when a new cycle begins. Because IDs go up in order, a replay's ID tells us roughly when it was played, even when we didn't collect it live.

Cycle 6 started around May 11, 2026, but our collector began on May 14 and then caught up on the replays it had missed. Cycle 5 replays came from an archive we imported all at once, so their saved date is just the import day.

How estimates are calculated

We start from the cycle's estimated start date, end date and total number of replays. Then we spread those replay IDs across the cycle's days, giving busy days more replays than quiet ones, and look up which day a replay's ID falls on.

"Busy" comes from what we measured in cycle 6: bonk.io is busiest on school-day afternoons, about 35% quieter on weekends during the school year, and quieter again in summer.

How accurate is it? We tested the method on cycle 6, where we know the real dates. Estimates were 2.4 days off on average, and 90% were within 5 days. Cycle 5 estimates are a bit less certain because the cycle's dates are estimates too, which is why we show the week rather than the day.

Known gaps

  • Our cycle 5 archive ends around early January 2026. Replays from roughly January 9 to April 29, 2026 aren't on Bonkverse.
  • Cycles 1–4 aren't on Bonkverse yet.
  • We miss about 15% of replays, mostly during short pauses when bonk.io limits how fast we can collect. They're spread evenly, not in big holes.

Have cycle 5 replays from after early January 2026, or a replay with a known date (a tournament, a video, a Discord post)? Let us know in the Bonkverse Discord. It helps fill gaps and makes the estimates more accurate.
